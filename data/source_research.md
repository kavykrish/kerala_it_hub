## 5. Private IT Institutes

Private institutes will NOT be hard-coded into the system.

They will be discovered dynamically through web search.

The system should prefer:

1. Official institute website
2. Official course page
3. Official admission page

Third-party listings will be used mainly for discovery or cross-checking.

---

## 6. Third-Party Education Platforms

Third-party education platforms may be used for:

- Course discovery
- Finding additional institutes
- Cross-checking information

Important information should preferably be verified against the official source.

---

## 7. Information to Extract

For every relevant course, attempt to retrieve:

- Institute name
- Course name
- Course category
- Duration
- Eligibility
- Fees
- Location
- Learning mode
- Curriculum
- Certification
- Admission information
- Course status
- Official website
- Source URL

---

## 8. Source Reliability Rules

- Prefer official government sources.
- Prefer official university sources for university programmes.
- Prefer official institute websites for private courses.
- Preserve the source URL.
- Do not invent missing information.
- If information is unavailable, clearly state that it was not found.
- Verify changing information from the official source.
